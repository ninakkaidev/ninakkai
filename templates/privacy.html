Privacy Policy

Date: 28/08/2025

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide
Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, photos, bio, preferences, etc.

Profile Content: Photos, videos, location, interests, and other profile-related data.

Messages and interactions — including chats, likes, matches, and all in-app communications — are fully encrypted in our database and are never used for any commercial purposes.

Payment Information: When purchasing premium features (handled by third-party processors).

b. Information We Collect Automatically
Device Information: IP address, device ID, operating system, app version.

Usage Data: App activity, pages viewed, features used, time spent, crash logs.

Location Data: If enabled, we collect precise or approximate location data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Create and manage your account.

Connect you with potential matches.

Personalize user experience and match suggestions.

Enable communication with other users.

Improve, test, and monitor the performance of the app.

Send updates, promotions, or important notifications.

Ensure safety, security, and compliance with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Your Information

We may share your data:

With other users: Based on your profile and app settings.

With service providers: Hosting, analytics, payment processing, customer support, etc.

For legal reasons: To comply with laws, enforce our terms, or protect our users.

In business transfers: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

Access the personal data we hold about you.

Correct, delete, or restrict your data.

Withdraw consent at any time.

Delete your account and data via app settings.

We retain your information as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide services. Upon deletion, we may retain some data for legal and safety reasons.

We take appropriate security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Use the app at your own risk.
