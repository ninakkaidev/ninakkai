About Ninakkai

In today's dating world, finding a genuine connection has become harder than ever. Most apps focus on swipes, appearances, or casual interactions, leaving people frustrated and unsure if anyone is truly real. That's the problem we wanted to solve.

Ninakkai was created to bring authenticity back into dating. Our solution starts with understanding you — not just your profile, but your heart and character.

We begin with 10 thoughtfully designed emotional questions that reveal your core personality, followed by additional questions that uncover your unique traits. On top of that, our Keeper/Seeker questions help identify your relationship readiness.

By combining all these insights, Ninakkai maps out who you truly are and connects you with people whose personalities, values, and emotional styles align with yours. This ensures every match isn't just a surface-level connection, but a meaningful, compatible partnership — built on sincerity, understanding, and real emotional compatibility.

With Ninakkai, dating becomes more than just meeting someone new; it becomes a journey to find the person who genuinely fits your life.
